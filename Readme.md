## 🏰 About the Game

You're the **royal mouser**, The MeowLord — the terror of rats.
You've sharpened your claws and readied your yarn.
They're infesting the palace, and it's your royal duty to make them vanish.

**MeowLord** is a fast-paced 2D action game where you play as a cat on a mission to clear the palace of its rat infestation. Jump across platforms, hunt down enemies, and restore order to the kingdom!

🎮 **[Play on itch.io](https://firefliesjar.itch.io/meowlord)**

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| **Jump** | Left click on an available platform |
| **Camera Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` / Arrow keys |
| **Center Camera** | Right click (centers on the cat) |
